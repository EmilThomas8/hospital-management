## Endpoints (sample)
- Admissions: `POST /api/admissions/admit`, `POST /api/admissions/:id/discharge`, `GET /api/admissions`.
- Rooms/Beds: `GET/POST/PUT/DELETE /api/rooms`, `/api/beds`.
- EMR: `POST /api/emr`, `GET /api/emr/patient/:patientId`.
- Billing: `POST /api/billing/:id/claim` (insurance submission).
- Discharge: `GET /api/discharge/:id/summary`.
- Reporting: `GET /api/reporting/kpis`, `GET /api/reporting/revenue/monthly`, `GET /api/reporting/occupancy`.
